## Official & Planning

Essential official tourism portals, entry requirements, and national weather forecasting.

- [VisitDenmark](https://www.visitdenmark.com/) - Official national tourist board portal featuring destination guides, seasonal itineraries, and cultural highlights.
- [New to Denmark (SIRI)](https://www.nyidanmark.dk/) - Official Danish immigration portal for visa requirements, entry rules, and residency guidelines.
- [DMI Weather (Danmarks Meteorologiske Institut)](https://www.dmi.dk/) - Official national meteorological institute offering weather forecasts, wind warnings, and radar maps.

## Transportation

Navigating train lines, regional ferries, public transit cards, and air travel across Denmark.

- [DSB (Danish State Railways)](https://www.dsb.dk/en/) - National railway service operating intercity trains, regional lines, and cross-border connections.
- [Rejseplanen](https://www.rejseplanen.dk/) - Comprehensive national public transport planner covering all trains, buses, metros, and local ferries.
- [DOT (Din Offentlige Transport)](https://dinoffentligetransport.dk/) - Integrated public transport authority for Greater Copenhagen's metro, buses, and harbor boats.
- [Molslinjen](https://www.molslinjen.com/) - Major ferry operator connecting Jutland and Zealand across Kattegat.
- [Copenhagen Airport (CPH)](https://www.cph.dk/en) - Primary international aviation hub providing rail and metro connections to central Copenhagen.

## Destinations & Regions

### Copenhagen & Capital Region

- [Wonderful Copenhagen](https://www.wonderfulcopenhagen.com/) - Official tourism organization for the capital region, highlighting neighborhoods, dining, and cultural events.
- [VisitNordsjælland](https://www.visitnordsjaelland.com/) - Visitor guide for North Zealand's royal castles, including Kronborg and Frederiksborg.

### Funen & Central Denmark

- [VisitFyn](https://www.visitfyn.com/) - Regional guide for Odense (Hans Christian Andersen's hometown), castles, and island cycling routes.
- [VisitAarhus](https://www.visitaarhus.com/) - Official tourist guide for Aarhus, featuring the ARoS art museum, Old Town open-air museum, and coastal paths.

### Jutland & West Coast

- [VisitVesterhavet](https://www.visitvesterhavet.com/) - Travel portal for the rugged Danish West Coast, beaches, and World War II bunker history.
- [VisitSkagen](https://www.skagentourist.dk/) - Official guide to Denmark's northernmost tip where the North Sea and Baltic Sea meet.

## Cycling & Outdoor Networks

Resources for exploring Denmark's national bicycle routes and protected nature areas.

- [National Cycle Routes (Naturstyrelsen)](https://naturstyrelsen.dk/) - Official government guide mapping Denmark's extensive network of long-distance bicycle paths.
- [Shelter App (Brug Naturen)](https://udinaturen.dk/) - Official national portal and map for finding public wilderness shelters, campsites, and campfire sites.

## Digital Nomad & Remote Work

Resources for remote workers, freelancers, and long-term digital professionals in Denmark.

- [Work in Denmark](https://www.workindenmark.dk/) - Official government portal for international professionals looking at employment, taxation, and relocation rules.
- [Startup Village Copenhagen](https://www.startupvillage.dk/) - Community hub and coworking resource network for tech professionals and entrepreneurs in the capital.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on emergency contacts, maritime safety, and local regulations.

- [112 Denmark Emergency Services](https://politi.dk/) - Direct national emergency hotline for police, fire, and medical assistance.
- [TrygFonden Coastal Safety](https://www.trygfonden.dk/) - Lifeguard and beach safety guidelines for Danish coastlines and swimming spots.
